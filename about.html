01 — About

The person
behind the packets

I’m an IT Network Analyst and cybersecurity practitioner who enjoys turning messy infrastructure into calm, secure, well-documented systems.

I combine extensive hands-on industry experience with doctoral-level research to protect critical digital infrastructure. Currently pursuing a Ph.D. in Information Technology (Information Security focus), I specialize in hardening enterprise networks, enforcing strict regulatory compliance, and ensuring data integrity across high-stakes environments.

From configuring FortiGate firewalls and server rooms under HIPAA in the United States, to teaching ICT in Myanmar and leading peer support at James Cook University Singapore, my work sits where technology meets people. I hold an M.S. in Cybersecurity (GPA 3.89) from Webster University, and I’m currently pursuing a Ph.D. in Information Technology (Digital Forensics) alongside a Master of IT in Artificial Intelligence.

Outside of work I publish research, write books, build tools for the community through SHAHHUB, and share what I learn on Shah’s Haven.
